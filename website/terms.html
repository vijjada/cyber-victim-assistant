Cybridge is an assistive software tool designed to guide cybercrime victims in systematically extracting transaction identifiers (12-digit UTR numbers, UPI VPAs, debit timestamps) and preparing draft narratives. Cybridge does not represent law enforcement, does not possess statutory authority to freeze bank accounts, and does not replace official police reporting.
