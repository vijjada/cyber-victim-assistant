Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 1, 2026 | Effective Date: November 1, 2026

Cybridge ("Cyber Victim Assistant", package com.cybervictim.assistant) is an open-source, on-device mobile utility built to assist citizens dealing with financial cyber fraud. We operate under a fundamental commitment: your data belongs exclusively to you and remains on your physical smartphone.

1. On-Device Local Processing

Cybridge is engineered as a local-first application. When you upload payment screenshots, bank debit transaction alerts, or email files:

  • Optical Character Recognition (OCR) is performed entirely on your device using on-device Google ML Kit models.
  • Raw images, screenshot bitmaps, and original SMS messages are never uploaded to our servers, third-party cloud AI vendors, or analytics platforms.
  • Only extracted text strings necessary for filling the complaint form are processed in local memory.

2. Device Permissions and Usage

Cybridge requests only permissions strictly necessary to assist with urgent cyber fraud reporting:

  • SMS Read Permission (Optional): Used exclusively to search for official bank debit alerts (-HDFCBK, -SBINB, -ICICIB) to automatically extract transaction timestamps, 12-digit UTR numbers, and lost amounts with user consent.
  • Storage / Media Access: Used exclusively to allow you to select payment receipts and export the generated Complaint Pack PDF.
  • Microphone / Audio (Optional): Used exclusively to support the hands-free "Speak out" voice intake in 12 Indian languages using Android's native SpeechRecognizer. Audio streams are transcribed locally and never retained.

3. Data Storage and Hardware Encryption

All case facts, evidence items, and generated narratives are stored inside an encrypted Android Room SQLite database on your device's private storage sandbox. Sensitive tokens are encrypted using hardware-backed AES-256 GCM encryption via Android Keystore.

4. Zero Third-Party Advertising & Tracking

Cybridge contains zero advertising SDKs, zero marketing tracking pixels, and zero third-party commercial analytics. We do not monetize your data in any manner.

5. Statutory Disclaimer

Cybridge is an independent assistance tool and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Government of India, I4C, or the Ministry of Home Affairs. It helps you prepare information; you submit your complaint yourself on the official portal (cybercrime.gov.in) or by calling 1930. This is not legal advice.
